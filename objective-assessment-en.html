1. Shared ruler

Sky-fitting, continuum geometry, and cosmogenesis-from-a-source are different questions. A result that answers one question is not a score on the others. This page uses that split for QNM and for its neighbours alike.

  • ΛCDM is the empirical low-energy baseline that any cosmogenesis proposal must recover, not a weak “pure-fit” cosmogenesis competitor.
  • Inflation is separated from ΛCDM. Its near-scale-invariant red spectrum is a major result. Potential and initial-condition freedom remain.
  • Causal sets receive credit for sequential growth and for the early heuristic order-of-magnitude estimate Λ ∼ 1/√V. That estimate is not a complete prediction from a finished quantum dynamics, and the coupling in covariant sequential growth remains free.
  • CDT receives credit for reproducible dynamical geometry and spectral-dimension flow. Four-dimensional building blocks and topology are inputs. A completed continuum-plus-realistic-perturbation chain is not yet delivered.
  • GFT-like programmes often have denser pregeometric dynamics shape. That is a real neighbour strength on that axis, not a score QNM may take by renaming.
  • QNM internal audit density is a methodological strength. It is not physical evidence that a cosmogenic mechanism is true.

6. Is continued research justified?

Yes, on a bounded reading, for QNM and for the neighbours alike. A classified source-cut and a stack of scoped theorems are enough to keep working. They are not enough to say that cosmogenesis is close. The same sentence applies to causal-set sequential growth and to CDT’s dynamical geometry: deposited objects justify continuation; they do not close the field.

QNM work should continue while it can deliver a later-layer constructor that is not already implied by the covering kernel, a non-fabricated issuance law, or a scoped negative theorem with independent mathematical value. Renaming free couplings, initial states, or protocol constants is a stop condition. CDT, causal sets, inflation, and ΛCDM should be read by their own deposits on the same split, not as trophies or as foils.

Two layers, not one. A research direction can already be a citable mathematical object. Cosmogenesis closure is a later event. Those two layers should not be welded — for QNM, or for anyone else on this page.
