4. Eighteen parameters: near-core vs far

Parameters fall into four groups: primordial → background → acoustic → structure/ionization. Do not read official acceptance tallies as “all eighteen are generated.”

4.1 Comparatively nearer “near-core” (few)

  • In-chain effective central charge / shape kernel (most stable engineering segment)
  • Scalar spectral index ns (among the shortest calibration paths)
  • Amplitude As on the shape side (shape witnessed; absolute scale still a debt)
  • Some acoustic geometric ratios (geometric chain exists; non-unique readout demotes claims)

4.2 Clearly farther / high-risk

  • H0, w0/wa, cosmic age: late-time; generative forward paths are honestly refuted or demoted in sectors
  • Tensor ratio r and some density parameters: often N-mixed / branch-rich — must not be folded into the scalar 15/17 headline
  • σ8 / S8: include disclosed structural ansatz — effective description, not unique upstream export
Public tallies to remember:
  • Pipeline A scalar acceptance commonly quoted as 15/17 (@21, r excluded)
  • A separate production-dictionary layer: 17/17 (@63) — different acceptance layers; never merge into “18/18 generated”
