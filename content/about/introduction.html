About the Quantum Narrative School

Curator’s formation, teaching lineage, and standing invitation. Not an official organ of the Accademia di Belle Arti di Venezia.

The Quantum Narrative School is a research community for work on the origin of the universe and on quantum-related questions. Independent researchers are welcome to join and to deposit papers.

The curator is Nanjie Ma (马楠杰). Correspondence: phoenix-mx@hotmail.com. ORCID: 0009-0002-4415-1209.

He trained at the Accademia di Belle Arti di Venezia (Venice Academy of Fine Arts). His painting teacher was Prof. Igor Lecic, holder of the painting chair. His sculpture teacher was Prof. Giuseppe La Bruna, holder of the sculpture chair. The history and methodology of art criticism, and contemporary art history, were studied under Prof. Saverio Simi De Burgis (1958–2026). That formation is artistic: studio practice together with the history and criticism of art. It is not a physics degree, and it is not claimed as one.

He does not present himself as a professionally credentialled physicist. After Venice he continued by independent study, with assistance from Cursor and other AI tools for reading, writing, and carrying out the work. Hosts and readers are asked to judge the deposited materials and the named unpaid objects, not to treat an art diploma as a physics degree or as a substitute for one.

The same years of studio teaching left a lasting question about how a world can be generated, constrained, and read. That question, rather than a commission from the Accademia, is why the present research exists. Any results this programme may obtain are offered as a fruit of that teaching—as an outcome of the education received in Venice—without converting the Accademia into the author of the scientific claims.

He holds that art and physics share a common root, both bound to the origin of the universe, and that this root is mathematics and information. He does not hold that human beings have free will: art, persons, and human civilisation are, in essence, the transfer of information.

This website and the Zenodo community “Quantum Narrative School” are maintained by the curator. They are not official organs of the Accademia di Belle Arti di Venezia. Naming Prof. Lecic, Prof. La Bruna, and Prof. Simi De Burgis records a teacher–student relationship. It does not imply that they, or the Accademia, endorse, supervise, or publish the research deposited here.

工作室教学留下的问题是：一个世界如何被生成、被约束、被读出。由此才有现在的研究，而不是美院的委托课题。若有成果，作为那一阶段受教育与师承的延伸献出；这不是把美院写成这些科学主张的作者。
