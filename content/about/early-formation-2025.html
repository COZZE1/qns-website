Early formation (2025)

Studio vocabulary · 12 September 2026 note · Nanjie Ma · origin of the name, not a payment notice

The Quantum Narrative School did not begin as a cosmology deposit. In 2025, after studio training at the Accademia di Belle Arti di Venezia, a working vocabulary took shape in painting and related cross-media work. That vocabulary is why the School has this name. It is not a commission from the Accademia, and it is not a physics degree. Artistic formation and the later claim boundary are stated on the Full About page.

The sequence recorded in those studio notes ran from painting concerned with repair, through a crayon-and-pencil studio series, to eight named themes. They were tools for asking how a world, a body, an interface, or a story can be generated, constrained, and read. They sit closer to art, interface, and analogy than to laboratory cosmology. They were not, and are not, a covering-kernel derivation of the universe.

The eight names are kept here as historical working vocabulary, not as paid physics:

  1. Synaptic cybernetics (突触控制论). Control and plasticity spoken as a working hypothesis, not as a measured quantum-biological fact.
  2. Screen shaman (屏幕萨满). Interface, attention, and cultural symbol as a readable surface.
  3. Tactile sovereignty (触觉主权). Who holds control of tactile interaction in a digital setting.
  4. Entropy democracy (熵民主). Decision and consensus spoken in an information-entropy vocabulary.
  5. Topological time (拓扑时间). Historical and narrative order spoken in a topological vocabulary.
  6. Mathematical-neural lexicon (数学神经词典). An attempt to give shared names to neural and mathematical structures.
  7. Quantum Narrative Matrix (量子叙事矩阵). In 2025 this was a tensor / narrative working image. It is not the later covering-kernel cosmogenesis programme that now uses the same public mark.
  8. Cellular-molecular metabolic politics (细胞分子代谢政治学). A systems-biology analogy for flow and constraint, not a derivation of political history.

Those early notes mixed operable remarks, metaphor, and formulas marked as still to be adjusted. They are not the live scientific record of this site. This page does not republish those essays, and it does not ask a reader to treat them as laboratory results.

Later independent work on cosmogenesis and discrete sequential growth (the covering-kernel QNM / QNSG programme) is a different object. Readers should not weld the 2025 names onto unpaid source-layer mouths, and should not read the later programme as having paid the 2025 essays. What should be judged in the later work remains the deposits, the named unpaid objects, and the disclosure registers.

量子叙事学派并不是从一笔宇宙学存款开始的。2025 年，在威尼斯美术学院的工作室训练之后，绘画与相关跨媒介工作里长出一套工作词汇。学派用这个名字，是因为那套词汇。它不是美院的委托课题，也不是物理学学位。造型培养与后来的主张边界见 Full About。

工作室笔记里的顺序是：与修复有关的绘画，经过一套蜡笔与彩铅的工作室系列，再到八个被点名的主题。它们是用来问：一个世界、一具身体、一个界面或一个故事，如何被生成、被约束、被读出。它们更接近艺术、界面与类比，而不是实验室宇宙学。它们当时不是、现在也不是覆盖核对宇宙的导出。

下面八个名字作为历史工作词汇保留，不是已付物理：

  1. 突触控制论（Synaptic cybernetics）。把控制与可塑性说成工作假说，不是已测到的量子生物学事实。
  2. 屏幕萨满（Screen shaman）。界面、注意与文化符号作为可读表面。
  3. 触觉主权（Tactile sovereignty）。数字环境里谁掌握触觉交互的控制。
  4. 熵民主（Entropy democracy）。用信息熵的词汇说决策与共识。
  5. 拓扑时间（Topological time）。用拓扑的词汇说历史与叙事秩序。
  6. 数学神经词典（Mathematical-neural lexicon）。给神经结构与数学结构找共用名称的一次尝试。
  7. 量子叙事矩阵（Quantum Narrative Matrix）。在 2025 年这是张量 / 叙事的工作图像。不是后来使用同一公开标识的覆盖核宇宙发生学纲领。
  8. 细胞分子代谢政治学（Cellular-molecular metabolic politics）。用系统生物学类比流与约束，不是对政治史的导出。

那些早期笔记里，可运算的句子、隐喻、以及标明仍待调整的公式是混在一起的。它们不是本站现行科学记录。本页不重发那些长文，也不请读者把它们当成实验室结果。

后来关于宇宙发生学与离散序贯生长的独立工作（覆盖核 QNM / QNSG 纲领）是另一个对象。请不要把 2025 年的名字焊到未付的源层对象上，也不要把后来的纲领读成已经付清了 2025 年的那些文章。后来的工作仍只核存款、点名的未付对象与披露登记。
