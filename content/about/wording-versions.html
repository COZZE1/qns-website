Wording, versions, and deposits

12 September 2026 · Nanjie Ma · live registers take precedence · not a payment notice

Some historical pages, visualizations, and related deposits used phrases such as “first principles” or “derived” more loosely than the live unpaid-mouths bar. Where an older sentence and a live page or machine-readable register disagree, the live page and the registers take precedence. That is a wording and version rule. It is not a claim that the older materials are paid cosmogenesis, and it is not a claim that the live unpaid objects have been paid.

When wording is found to over-state what is paid, later versions will correct it. Older visualizations remain historical demonstrations unless a live page says otherwise. Hardcoded or conventional fills in those demonstrations are not to be read as deposited payment.

If historical wording exceeded the objects that are paid, the correction is the later version. Please read the claim boundary on each page, the named unpaid objects, and the deposits.

Zenodo deposits under the Quantum Narrative School community continue. Packaging a journal submission is not the present priority. That pause is not a hold release, not a staged-breakthrough notice, and not a request that a reader wait for a journal version before judging the live registers.
